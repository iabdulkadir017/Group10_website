Welcome

Building Nigeria’s Computer Engineering Capacity

The Department of Computer Engineering at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria trains engineers across the full spectrum of computing hardware and software from digital systems and embedded intelligence to networks, security and artificial intelligence. The department is committed to imparting Computer Engineering knowledge and skills at all levels to serve the needs of the nation and humanity.

Housed in a dedicated departmental building on the Samaru main campus, the department offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes supported by dedicated laboratories, an experienced academic and technical staff complement, and active research groups spanning artificial intelligence, networks and security, and embedded systems.

Departmental Focus

Six Areas of Focus

Our teaching and research activities are organised around six broad focus areas that reflect the breadth of modern computer engineering.

01

Information/Computer Technology and Systems

Core computing infrastructure, systems software and information technology practice.

02

Network and Security

Computer networks, communications and the security of digital systems.

03

Artificial Intelligence

Intelligent algorithms, machine learning and knowledge-based systems.

04

Control and Intelligent Systems

Automatic control theory and intelligent automation of physical processes.

05

Embedded Systems

Microprocessor-based and embedded hardware/software system design.

06

Image Processing and Computer Vision

Digital image analysis, computer vision and visual pattern recognition.

B.Eng. Specializations

Undergraduate Specializations

Within the B.Eng. Computer Engineering programme, students may focus their advanced studies in one of three specialization areas distinct from the six broader departmental focus areas above.

Specialization

Computer Systems and Networks

Computing hardware, systems architecture, and networked and communication infrastructure.

Specialization

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, intelligent agents and knowledge-based system design.

Specialization

Control and Intelligent Systems

Automatic control, robotics and intelligent automation of engineering systems.
