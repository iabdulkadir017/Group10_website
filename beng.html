B.Eng. Computer Engineering

A five-year Bachelor of Engineering programme preparing students for professional practice across the full breadth of computer engineering.

5

Duration

Five years of full-time study, according to departmental programme information.

3

Specializations

Computer Systems and Networks, Artificial Intelligence, and Control and Intelligent Systems.

FYP

Final-Year Project

A capstone engineering project undertaken in the final year of study.

Programme Overview

Overview

The B.Eng. Computer Engineering programme develops graduates capable of analysing, designing, developing, testing and producing computer engineering products and services. The programme integrates core engineering science with specialised computer engineering training in digital and embedded systems, networks and security, and artificial intelligence and control.

Course Structure

Specializations

Specialization Tracks

Computer Systems and Networks

Computing hardware, systems architecture, and networked and communication infrastructure.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, intelligent agents and knowledge-based system design.

Control and Intelligent Systems

Automatic control, robotics and intelligent automation of engineering systems.
