M.Sc. Computer Engineering

Advanced graduate study and research in specialised areas of computer engineering.

Programme Overview

Overview

The M.Sc. Computer Engineering programme combines advanced coursework with a research component, allowing candidates to specialise in areas such as artificial intelligence, networks and security, embedded systems, or image processing and computer vision, in line with the department’s research groups.

Specific current details such as duration, admission requirements, thesis requirements and fees for the M.Sc. programme should be confirmed directly with the department, as this information is subject to periodic review.

Research Alignment

Postgraduate study aligns with the department’s active research groups: the AI, Robotics and Control Systems Group (ARCSG), the Network and Security Group (NSG), the Embedded Systems and Applications Development Group (ESADG), and the Image Processing and Computer Vision Group (PICVG).
