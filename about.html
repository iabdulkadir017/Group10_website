About the Department

The story, philosophy and guiding objectives of the Department of Computer Engineering, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

Overview

About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering is part of the Faculty of Engineering at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. It is dedicated to imparting Computer Engineering knowledge and skills at all levels undergraduate through doctoral to serve the needs of the nation and humanity. The department trains engineers across computing hardware and software, networks and security, artificial intelligence, control and intelligent systems, embedded systems, and image processing and computer vision.

Our Story

History

The Department of Computer Engineering traces its origins through the evolution of engineering education at Ahmadu Bello University.

  1. 1962 — Ahmadu Bello University is established.
  2. Founding years — Engineering education at ABU is inherited from the Nigeria College of Arts, Science and Technology, Zaria, initially offering Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering.
  3. 1965 — The Faculty of Engineering becomes autonomous.
  4. Subsequent years — The former Electrical Engineering Department evolves over time as the scope of electrical and computing disciplines expands.
  5. 2010/11 — Following NUC approval, the programme changes from Electrical Engineering to Electrical and Computer Engineering.
  6. Later evolution — The combined programme subsequently evolves into separate departments, including the Department of Computer Engineering.
  7. November 2017 — The three resulting departments obtain full autonomy.
Our Philosophy

Philosophy

The department is focused on imparting Computer Engineering and Technology knowledge and skills at all levels to serve the needs of the nation and humanity.

Mission

Our Mission

To provide quality, up-to-date education, research and community service in Computer Engineering that prepares graduates to design, develop and advance computing technology in service of the nation and humanity.

Vision

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in Computer Engineering education, research and innovation in Nigeria and beyond.

What We Aim For

Departmental Objectives

Objective 01

Provide regular and updated programmes and courses in all fields of Computer Engineering to the standard expected of a high-standing department and university.

Objective 02

Educate and equip students with the technical knowledge, skills and competencies required to analyse, design, develop, test and produce Computer Engineering products and services.

Objective 03

Promote research and advancement in all fields of Computer Engineering.

Objective 04

Promote the diffusion of Computer Engineering knowledge throughout Nigeria and the world.
