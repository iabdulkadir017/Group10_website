Students

Resources and information for current students of the Department of Computer Engineering.

Guidance

Academic Advisers

Each student is supported by an academic adviser drawn from the department’s academic staff, who provides guidance on course registration, academic progress and general departmental matters. Individual adviser assignments are managed internally by the department and are not published on this website.

SWEP

The Student Work Experience Programme (SWEP) exposes students to practical work experience at an appropriate stage of the B.Eng. programme, complementing classroom instruction with hands-on engineering exposure.

SIWES

The Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) places students in industry at the 400 Level, providing an extended period of supervised industrial training relevant to computer engineering practice.

Capstone

Final Year Projects

Final-year students undertake an individual engineering project under the supervision of an academic staff member, culminating in a written thesis and project defence/exhibition, as prescribed by departmental regulations.
