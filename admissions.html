Admissions

Undergraduate, Direct Entry and postgraduate admission pathways into the Department of Computer Engineering.

Admission requirements are subject to current university and regulatory guidelines. Applicants should confirm the latest requirements through official ABU admission channels.
Undergraduate

Undergraduate Admission (UTME/PUTME)

O’Level Requirements

Five SSCE/NECO credits, including:

  • Mathematics
  • English Language
  • Physics
  • Chemistry

UTME/PUTME Subjects

Relevant UTME subjects include:

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
Direct Entry

Direct Entry Admission

A-Level Pathway

A-Level passes in Physics and Mathematics.

OND/HND Pathway

Relevant OND/HND qualifications in a related field.

Postgraduate

Postgraduate Admission

Admission into the PGD, M.Sc. and Ph.D. Computer Engineering programmes follows the general postgraduate admission requirements of Ahmadu Bello University, alongside departmental requirements specific to Computer Engineering. Please confirm current requirements, application deadlines and fees with the department.
