Research & Innovation

The department’s research activity spans six focus areas and is coordinated through four active research groups.

Focus Areas

Research & Focus Areas

01

Information/Computer Technology and Systems

Core computing infrastructure, systems software and information technology practice.

02

Network and Security

Computer networks, communications and the security of digital systems.

03

Artificial Intelligence

Intelligent algorithms, machine learning and knowledge-based systems.

04

Control and Intelligent Systems

Automatic control theory and intelligent automation of physical processes.

05

Embedded Systems

Microprocessor-based and embedded hardware/software system design.

06

Image Processing and Computer Vision

Digital image analysis, computer vision and visual pattern recognition.

Research Groups

Our Research Groups

Departmental research activity is coordinated through the following groups.

ARCSG

AI, Robotics and Control Systems Group

Research spanning artificial intelligence, robotics and control systems engineering.

NSG

Network and Security Group

Research in computer networks, communications and information security.

ESADG

Embedded Systems and Applications Development Group

Research and development in embedded systems and their applications.

PICVG

Image Processing and Computer Vision Group

Research in digital image processing and computer vision.

Individual research projects, publications and group memberships are maintained by each research group. contact the department for current research group activity.

Our M.Sc. and Ph.D. programmes align directly with these research groups.
