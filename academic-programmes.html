Academic Programmes

Undergraduate and postgraduate study pathways offered by the Department of Computer Engineering.

Undergraduate

B.Eng. Computer Engineering

A five-year programme covering digital systems, embedded systems, networks and security, artificial intelligence, and control and intelligent systems, culminating in a final-year project.

  • Duration: five years
  • Includes SWEP and SIWES
  • Three specialization tracks

PGD Computer Engineering
