Facilities

The departmental building houses laboratories, lecture halls, a departmental library and staff offices across four floors.

Building Layout

Floor-by-Floor Layout

The departmental building is organised as follows.

Ground Floor

Laboratories

Departmental laboratories supporting practical and project-based learning.

1st Floor

Lecture Halls

300 Level, 400 Level and 500 Level lecture halls and library.

2nd Floor

Departmental Staff Offices

The department’s academic and administrative staff offices.

3rd Floor

Postgraduate Lecture Hall

Dedicated lecture hall for postgraduate instruction.

Ground Floor

Laboratories

The department’s laboratories occupy the ground floor of the building, providing hands-on, practical training and project space for students and staff across the department’s focus areas.

First Floor

Lecture Halls

The first floor houses the department’s library and undergraduate lecture halls, where core departmental courses are delivered to the 300, 400 and 500 Level classes.

1st Floor

300 Level Lecture Hall

Lecture hall for 300 Level departmental courses.

1st Floor

400 Level Lecture Hall

Lecture hall for 400 Level departmental courses.

1st Floor

500 Level Lecture Hall

Lecture hall for 500 Level departmental courses.
